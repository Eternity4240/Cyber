Cybersecurity Awareness

Understand The Threats

Cybersecurity is more than antivirus software. Everyday decisions such as clicking links, choosing passwords and sharing information can have a major impact on your digital security.

Phishing

Phishing attacks use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing passwords, financial information or other sensitive data.

Look for unusual senders, urgent requests, suspicious links and unexpected attachments.

Password Security

Weak or reused passwords can allow attackers to gain access to multiple accounts if one password is compromised.

Use unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication where possible.

Malware

Malware is malicious software that can damage systems, steal information, monitor activity or disrupt normal computer operation.

Avoid unknown downloads and keep operating systems and security software updated.

Online Scams

Scammers often create fake offers, fake support messages and fake websites to convince people to send money or information.

Be especially careful with unexpected requests for payments or personal details.

Social Engineering

Social engineering attacks target people rather than directly attacking technology. Attackers use trust, fear or urgency to manipulate victims.

Verify unusual requests before sharing information or taking action.

Safe Browsing

Websites and downloads can contain malicious content or misleading information. Safe browsing reduces the chance of accidentally interacting with threats.

Check website addresses and avoid suspicious downloads and pop-ups.

Account Security

Protect important accounts using strong authentication methods and secure recovery options.

Enable MFA and review account security settings regularly.

Privacy

Personal information shared online can sometimes be collected, misused or combined with other information.

Share only what is necessary and review privacy settings on important services.

Backups

Backups provide another copy of important files if a device fails, files are accidentally deleted or malware affects the system.

Keep important files backed up using a reliable storage method.
Technology Helpdesk

Fix Your Technology

Many computer problems have simple causes. Understanding basic troubleshooting can help identify the problem before more complicated solutions are needed.

Wi-Fi & Internet

Troubleshoot slow or disconnected internet, Wi-Fi connection failures and devices that cannot access the network.

Check the connection, restart networking equipment and verify network settings.

Slow Computer

Slow performance can be caused by too many background applications, limited storage, outdated software or insufficient resources.

Check startup programs, available storage and running applications.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth devices may fail to connect because of pairing issues, disabled settings, interference or outdated drivers.

Check Bluetooth settings and remove and re-pair the device when necessary.

Printers

Printer problems can involve connection issues, incorrect printer selection, driver problems or stuck print jobs.

Check the connection, printer status, queue and selected printer.

Storage

Running out of storage can slow a device and prevent applications or updates from working correctly.

Identify large files and unnecessary applications before removing anything.

Software Problems

Applications may freeze, crash or refuse to open because of corrupted files, outdated versions or system conflicts.

Restart the application, check updates and investigate error messages.

Audio Problems

Missing or distorted audio can be caused by incorrect output devices, muted settings, drivers or connected peripherals.

Check volume, output device and connection settings first.

Keyboard & Mouse

Input devices may stop working because of batteries, cables, Bluetooth pairing, USB connections or driver issues.

Try another port or reconnect the device before changing advanced settings.

Overheating

Excessive heat can reduce performance and potentially damage hardware if the problem continues.

Keep vents clear, use the device on a hard surface and avoid blocking airflow.
